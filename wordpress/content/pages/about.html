WanderMetric is a booking guide for travellers. For each tour, ticket, transfer, eSIM or car hire on the site, we read the partner’s own listing and write down what you need to know before you pay: where to meet, what is and is not included, how cancellation works, and what to watch for.

How a page is made

  • We read the listing on the partner’s own site, never a reseller’s copy of it.
  • We write down what matters: meeting points, opening hours, what is included, the cancellation rules, and anything that catches people out.
  • We date it. Every page names the listing it was read from and the day it was read. Prices and rules change, so check the partner’s page before you book; the booking button takes you there.

What a page is not

A page is not a review. Unless it says otherwise, we have not taken the tour or used the service ourselves: we describe the listing carefully and point out what to check. Any rating shown belongs to the partner’s site and is labelled as such.

How we are funded

We earn commission when a reader books through a partner link on this site. That funding model only works if the pages are worth trusting, so it is kept apart from the writing: commission rates never decide what we cover or what we say about it.

Every page carrying affiliate links says so, in context, near the links themselves. We do not accept payment for coverage, and we do not publish sponsored posts dressed up as editorial.

What we will not do

  • Publish invented prices, availability, ratings or reviews. If we do not hold the data, we do not show a number.
  • Mass-generate thin pages to chase search traffic. An offer page cannot be published here until it carries real, original substance — that rule is enforced by the publishing system, not by good intentions.
  • Recommend something because it pays better.
