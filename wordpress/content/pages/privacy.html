This policy describes what this site actually does. It is written against the implementation rather than copied from a template.

What we collect

We do not record page views ourselves. When you follow a link to a booking partner, we record:

  • which offer the link belongs to, and the time of the click
  • the path of the page on this site you clicked from
  • the hostname of the site that referred you here, if any: the host only, never the full referring URL

That is how we know which pages lead to bookings, which is how this site is funded.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • Your IP address is never stored.
  • No cross-site tracking identifier, and no fingerprinting.
  • No name, email or other personal detail.
  • No advertising trackers.

Cookies and your choice

The cookie notice offers two choices, and we remember which one you made with a first-party cookie called wm_consent (and the same value in your browser’s local storage). It holds only “all” or “essential” and lasts 180 days. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Essential only: nothing is requested from any third party.

Accept all: we also load

  • partner search forms and widgets from Travelpayouts (tpemd.com) on the pages that carry them. They are run by the partner, set the partner’s own cookies and send your searches to the partner;
  • Google Analytics, to measure which pages are read, when it is switched on. Google signals and ad personalisation are off, so it is used for measurement only.

Fonts and images are served from this site, so no font or image request goes to a third party.

Affiliate links

When you follow a partner link, the partner’s own tracking credits any resulting booking to us. The partner then applies its own privacy policy, which we do not control. We never pass your personal details to a partner, because we do not hold them.

Newsletter

There is no newsletter sign-up at the moment. If we add one, it will be double opt-in, and this page will say exactly what it stores before it goes live.

Retention

Click records are deleted after 13 months.

Where it is stored

The site runs on WordPress, hosted by Hostinger. Click records are stored in the site’s own database there.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and equivalent laws you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold. In practice we hold none, since nothing we store identifies a person. Write to privacy@wandermetric.com.
